Ngiting Tagumpay: The Nationwide Oral Health Campaign

Launched on March 13, 2026 — just ahead of World Oral Health Day — Ngiting Tagumpay is a joint advocacy of PDA, PARDTI, and Haleon (makers of Sensodyne and Polident).

What the campaign delivers:

  • Free dental consultations in major cities
  • Oral health education outreach to communities
  • 1,500 free dentures for beneficiaries
  • Awareness through digital creators and media
— Dr. Niñal on the Campaign

“Many Filipinos tend to delay seeking dental care until symptoms become more difficult to manage. Through programs like Ngiting Tagumpay, we can reach more communities with reliable information while connecting them to proper dental care.”

“Our continued collaboration with Haleon reflects the value of working with partners who prioritize science-based solutions and responsible health advocacy.”

This builds on PDA and Haleon’s previous “Oplan Balik Ngiti” program, which provided denture assistance to 5,600 individuals in prior years.

Where the Campaign Will Go

Ngiting Tagumpay will travel to major cities across the country, targeting rural barangays, senior citizens, public school students, and low-income families who view dental care as a luxury expense.

The launch was supported by content creators Macoy Dubs and The Aguinaldos, plus host Tuesday Vargas — amplifying the message to younger Filipinos through social media.

Colgate–PDA: Year-Long Free Dental Check-Ups

In February 2026, Colgate-Palmolive Philippines and PDA launched a massive initiative: free dental check-ups for every Filipino who buys Colgate Maximum Cavity Protection.

  1. Buy any Colgate Maximum Cavity Protection product
  2. Scan the QR code on the pack
  3. Book a free check-up at an accredited PDA clinic

This is part of Colgate Philippines’ 100th anniversary celebration. Their commitment: 100 million oral health kits to Filipino children by 2035. The campaign launched with Kathryn Bernardo as brand ambassador.

Breaking the “Kaya Pa” Mindset

The campaign addresses a deeply rooted cultural barrier: the Filipino “Kaya pa” mindset — the natural resiliency that leads people to endure pain and treat dental health as secondary until it becomes an emergency.

— Why This Matters for Clinics

PDA-accredited clinics are the referral destinations for this campaign. If your clinic is registered, you get walk-in patients driven by a national advertising campaign featuring Kathryn Bernardo — for free.

Those walk-ins who come for a free check-up often become paying patients for restorations, orthodontics, and other treatments. The free check-up is the top of YOUR funnel.

What This Means for Philippine Dentistry

Under Dr. Niñal’s leadership, PDA’s 2026 initiatives share a common thread: making dental care accessible to more Filipinos while empowering the dentists who provide it.

Ngiting Tagumpay addresses the demand side. The Colgate partnership creates a sustainable pipeline of patients. The convention builds skills and connections. Together, they signal that a PDA driving more Filipinos to clinics creates more demand for everything those clinics need — chairs, instruments, imaging, sterilization, and consumables.
