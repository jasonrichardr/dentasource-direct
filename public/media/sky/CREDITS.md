# Deep-Sky Imagery Credits
- eso0932a-milkyway*.jpg — "The Milky Way panorama", ESO / Serge Brunier (GigaGalaxy Zoom), CC BY 4.0. https://www.eso.org/public/images/eso0932a/
  Real 360° photographic panorama of the night sky in galactic coordinates — every star a real photographed sun; the band is our own galaxy seen from inside (galactic centre ~26,000 ly); Magellanic Clouds ~160,000–200,000 ly; Andromeda ~2.5 million ly, at their TRUE angular sizes.
- carina-jwst-2048.jpg — "Cosmic Cliffs" in the Carina Nebula, NASA/ESA/CSA/STScI (James Webb Space Telescope, 2022). A stellar nursery ~7,600 light-years away. Public domain (NASA).
- pillars-jwst-2048.jpg — "Pillars of Creation" (NIRCam), NASA/ESA/CSA/STScI (JWST, 2022). Star-forming columns in the Eagle Nebula ~6,500 light-years away. Public domain (NASA).
- hudf-hubble-2048.jpg — Hubble deep field ("legion of galaxies"), NASA/ESA/Hubble. Nearly every point of light is an entire galaxy, billions of light-years away — the deepest kind of photograph humanity makes. Public domain (NASA).
